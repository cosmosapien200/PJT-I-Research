# Deviations from AGENT_PLAN.md and from the original papers (one line each: what, why)

## Data
- CirCor downloaded file-by-file from the PhysioNet S3 mirror instead of the zip: the physionet.org zip ran at a few kB/s.
- BMD-HS cloned from github.com/sani002/BMD-HS-Dataset: the URL in the plan/paper (mHealthBuet) returns "repository not found"; sani002 is the mirror listed on the lab's publications page.
- PASCAL B taken from the Kaggle mirror (kinguistics/heartbeat-sounds, `set_b`): the original istethoscope.peterjbentley.com host does not respond. Only labelled recordings used (461: 320 normal, 95 murmur, 46 extrasystole; unlabelled test files ignored). The noisy-normal / noisy-murmur sublabels are kept (column `sublabel`).
- PASCAL patient ID = the numeric subject token in the file name (e.g. `127` in `extrastole__127_...`); 165 patients.
- ZCHSound: the official site (zchsound.ncrcch.org.cn) did not respond; the dataset was taken from the user-supplied GitHub LFS mirror (WeiJieOvO/ZCHSound-Dataset). Only the "clean" subset (941 recordings with diagnoses) is used; the 318-recording noisy subset has no diagnosis column.
- ZCHSound has no patient IDs; each recording is treated as one patient (the paper reports ~one recording per participant).
- EPHNOGRAM (optional extra negatives) not used: optional, large download, and the common corpus already has 8.5k unusable windows.
- DEMAND: 16 kHz versions of 17 environments (SCAFE has no 16 kHz file on Zenodo), channel 1 only, cut into 30 s clips.
- LibriSpeech/Mini LibriSpeech utterances capped at 12 s (as in PCGKolect_model/seg/prep_noise.py).
- All noise clips are 20 Hz high-passed and scaled to unit RMS (as in PCGKolect_model/seg/prep_noise.py).
- PhysioNet split: StratifiedGroupKFold (5 folds, seed 42) stratified by abnormal x SQI, grouped by subject for subset b and by recording elsewhere (no subject IDs in a, c, d, e, f): records of one subject in subsets other than b may cross folds.
- PhysioNet noise stretch = from the `(N` marker to the `N)` marker (the repo's prep_seg.py also labels the segment after `N)` as noise).

## Training windows
- Augmented windows (30 % of usable windows + Bank 1 noise at U[-5, 5] dB) are added as extra copies; the clean originals are kept.
- Bank 1 clips are assigned to PhysioNet folds (noise stretches inherit their record's fold, other clips random) and a window is only augmented with a clip of its own fold, so no noise clip appears in both training and validation folds.
- Window audio is stored peak-normalised in float16; every model is gain-invariant (z-score / log-mean / ratio features).
- Log-mels for B4/B5/E6 are computed on the GPU from the cached audio at load time (deterministic) instead of being stored on disk (would be ~10 GB across all test conditions).

## Models
- B1: only the five SQIs listed in the plan (kurtosis, envelope std, max envelope autocorrelation at 0.5-2 s lag, envelope sample entropy at 30 Hz, pyPCG cyclostationary degree of periodicity); no further thesis SQIs. Band-pass 25-400 Hz Butterworth order 4 (zero-phase) is an assumption, not confirmed from the thesis. Envelope autocorrelation computed on the 20 Hz low-passed Hilbert envelope at 50 Hz.
- B1 SVM: trained on a stratified random subsample of 12,000 windows (seed 42) per fold / final model so that RBF-SVM + Platt probabilities stay tractable; LR uses all windows.
- B2: 11 of 17 features implemented: sample entropy of the processed autocorrelation (variant m=2, r=0.2·SD, at 50 Hz; the other 3 variants skipped), kurtosis and variance of the processed autocorrelation, min ratio s2/s1 of singular values (matrix of consecutive autocorrelation segments of length L, minimum over L = 0.4-1.5 s; our interpretation of the patent), signal power (mean square of the 20 Hz low-passed envelope of the z-scored window), correlation with a least-squares fit of 1, 2, 3 rectified sinusoids (harmonics of a fundamental grid-searched over 0.5-3 Hz), log power ratio 0-240 vs 240-1000 Hz, mean and variance of log(0-500 Hz / rest) over 2 s frames with 0.5 s overlap. Decaying-rectified-sinusoid features skipped (under-specified).
- B2: class-weighted single random forest instead of the original 16 balanced models averaged.
- B3 CardioPHON: the repository (git.lih.lu/vdespotovic/cardiophon) has no feature-extraction code and no trained quality model, so, at the user's request (overriding the plan's fallback), the 416-feature set was re-implemented from the column names of the repo's feature CSVs and the Grooby et al. feature descriptions (`src/cardiophon_features.py`). Definitions are approximations: time-domain/LPC/LSF/entropy, wavelet energies and entropies, YIN F0 statistics, truncated-autocorrelation entropies/peaks, Welch-spectrum shape incl. a 4-component GMM, two segmentations (our Springer LR-HSMM with per-window timing = 'Springer', with population timing = 'Schmidt-like', not a true Schmidt HSMM), 7 envelope types (var, sample entropy, cycle correlation, HR stability), PSD band ratios, 14 MFCC statistics and peak-window plausibility counts. Spectral bands above 500 Hz are empty at 1 kHz (constant features, never selected).
- B3 classifier follows the repo's published configuration (standardise, mutual-information top 20 % = 83 features, soft voting of grid-searched RBF-SVM + RF 200 + GB 200, no class balancing), with a median imputer, feature clipping at |z| <= 20, seed 42 instead of 1, and the SVM grid search/fit on a stratified 8,000-window subsample.
- B3 input: 5 s beat window -> 1 kHz -> extended to 6 s by concatenating copies (CardioPHON extends recordings shorter than 6 s). No as-published arm: the repo's training data are feature tables only (no audio-level model to apply to our test beats).
- B4/B5: 20 Hz high-pass = 2nd-order Butterworth (causal), as in the PCGKolect front-end. STFT: 50-sample (25 ms) Hann window zero-padded to 512 points, hop 20 (10 ms). Validation = PhysioNet fold 0 (~20 %, patient-grouped where IDs exist).
- E6 balanced CNN: balancing implemented by weighted sampling with replacement (usable windows from normal and abnormal recordings get equal total sampling weight, unusable windows unchanged) rather than discarding data.
- B6: each 5 s beat window is reflection-padded to the model's 10,328-sample input and decoded on its own, instead of running the streaming scheduler with 20 s history (one code path for E1 and E2; rhythm check therefore sees the ~4-6 beats in the window). Threshold confidence 0.80 (device default) used, README says 0.70.
- B6 window-level score on PhysioNet (Phase B table): 1 − mean beat confidence in the window (B6b: confidence set to 0 for beats failing duration/rhythm); 1 if no beat decoded. B6 was trained on all of PhysioNet, so its PhysioNet numbers are optimistic.
- B7 uses the reference segmentation positions (annotations, or the Springer segmenter run on the clean recording) also under added noise (oracle positions). On PhysioNet, windows without hand-corrected annotations (e.g. inside noise stretches) get score +50, so its Phase B numbers are optimistic. Score = −SNR dB (not in [0, 1]); threshold −14 corresponds to the published 14 dB.

## Test beats
- At most 8 beats per recording (random, seed from record ID) to keep E2 tractable.
- Beat-window and clean-rule SNR computed after a 25-400 Hz band-pass.
- Springer LR-HSMM (pyPCG port, hsmmlearn from GitHub) trained on 400 random SQI-1 PhysioNet records with hand-corrected S1/S2 centres; run at 1 kHz with per-record heart-rate re-estimation; beats with duration outside 0.3-2.0 s are discarded and counted as segmentation failures.
- CirCor patients with murmur Absent and outcome Abnormal (263 patients) form their own group `no_murmur_abnormal_outcome` (not covered by the plan's classes; not merged into normal).
- CirCor `murmur_diastolic` = any diastolic murmur at an audible location (only 5 patients; 4 also have a systolic murmur, whose beats have no murmur-free phase and use the quieter phase in the clean rule).
- Clean-rule murmur phase for diseases: AS/MR systolic, AR/MS diastolic, BMD multi = phases of its diseases; ZCHSound VSD/ASD systolic, PDA both (continuous), PFO unknown; PASCAL murmur unknown (quieter phase used when both or unknown).
- PASCAL: a patient counts as normal (calibration-eligible) only if all their recordings are normal.
- Synthetic rhythm sets: 300 clean normal CirCor evaluation recordings; beats concatenated to 20 s, durations changed only by trimming / extending (reflected tail) the diastole; premature beats shift 25-40 % of the previous RR earlier with a compensatory pause; up to 4 modified beats per sequence are scored. They use CirCor's thresholds and are compared with CirCor normal beats.
- E2 noise: Bank 2 source chosen 50/50 (LibriSpeech vs DEMAND) before picking the clip, otherwise 94 % of clips would be speech.

## Analysis
- Matched threshold ties: deep scores are computed in float64 (float32 sigmoids saturated at exactly 1.0) and beats exactly at the 90th-percentile threshold are rejected with the probability that makes calibration rejection exactly 10 % (deterministic per beat ID). Needed because B6 gives 1.0 to every unmatched beat (e.g. 15 % of ZCHSound calibration beats). Logged in ANALYSIS_PLAN.md.
- GEE: noise levels with no variation in a contrast (e.g. 100 % rejection at 0 dB) are dropped from that fit (complete separation); if the exchangeable fit does not converge, independence working correlation (still patient-clustered robust SEs) is used (18 of 162 fits).
- Bootstrap CIs for differences resample patients independently in the pathological and normal groups; synthetic rhythm beats come from CirCor evaluation patients who also contribute normal beats, so their CIs ignore that pairing.
- E5: early-stopping validation = 15 % of training patients (not specified in plan); sensitivity/specificity at 0.5 on the max-over-beats patient score; patients with murmur Unknown excluded.
- E3 "disease present" breakdown for BMD-HS counts multi-disease patients under each of their diseases (in addition to the class breakdown).
- E4 pools pathological classes as: CirCor systolic + diastolic murmur; ZCHSound ASD+VSD+PFO+PDA; BMD-HS AS+AR+MR+MS+multi; PASCAL murmur+extrasystole (unknown, other-location, no-murmur-abnormal-outcome and synthetic beats excluded).
- B6 at its published threshold rejects 27 % of clean ZCHSound control calibration beats and leaves 15 % of them unmatched (paediatric heart rates); reported as found.

## Follow-up fixes (2026-10-16)
- Fix 1: B1 SVM excluded from every CirCor table (and from E5). Not a bug: the scaler sits inside the sklearn Pipeline (fit on training folds, reused at test time) and the 4 kHz -> 2 kHz resampling passes 100/300 Hz and suppresses 1.5 kHz by ~30 dB. The Platt-scaled RBF-SVM output saturates near 0.75 (PhysioNet unusable windows: 90th percentile 0.765) and CirCor's matched threshold (0.72) lies at that ceiling, so added noise cannot raise rejection (0 dB: 18 % vs 81-93 % on the other datasets). Decision-function scores would not help (monotone transform). Figure: figures/fix1_b1svm_score_vs_snr_circor.png.
- Segmenter error found during the audit: pyPCG's Springer heart-rate search is capped at 120 bpm (and its systole search fails above ~150 bpm), which halved infant heart rates in ZCHSound (neonates 79 bpm). ZCHSound is now segmented with a 30-220 bpm range (systole search window shrunk to [0.25 RR, RR/2] when RR/2 < 0.2 s); BMD-HS and PASCAL (adults) keep 30-120 bpm, because the wide range locked onto the S1-S2 interval in ~15 % of PASCAL recordings. The range is chosen by population (known a priori), validated on held-out PhysioNet records only (results/fix3_segmenter_validation_physionet.csv); BMD-HS/PASCAL beats are bit-identical to the first analysis. All ZCHSound beats, windows, clean flags and scores were regenerated.
- Fix 2: pooled threshold = patient-weighted (each calibration patient weight 1) 90th percentile of clean normal calibration beats pooled over all four datasets, with the same tie randomisation as the matched threshold.
- Fix 3: heart rate per beat = 60 / beat duration from the segmentation (annotation for CirCor, Springer for the others; synthetic beats: their constructed durations). Age: CirCor age category; ZCHSound age in days binned into the same categories (Neonate < 28 d, Infant < 1 y, Child < 12 y, Adolescent); BMD-HS 10-year age bins from additional_metadata.csv; PASCAL has none. HR enters the GEE as a within-contrast z-score plus its square. Age levels with < 2 patients in a contrast are dropped from the age-adjusted fit. BMD-HS healthy subjects are all aged 20-29, so BMD-HS age adjustment is not identifiable and is not interpreted.
- Fix 4: every GEE row also reports the independence-working-correlation fit (or_indep, p_indep) as a sensitivity analysis; the exchangeable fit stays primary as in the plan. They disagree in direction in 30 of 209 main fits, all near-null except the PhysioNet-only B6 on CirCor systolic murmur (exchangeable OR 0.86, independence OR 1.33, p = 0.009).
- Fix 5 (E5): patient score = mean over beats; the decision threshold is Youden's J on the 15 % of training patients held out from fitting (never the test patients); 3 seeds (42, 43, 44) vary initialisation and batch order with fixed data splits.
- Fix 6: PCGKolect U-Net retrained with the repo's own recipe (seg/train_seg.py --final, 12,000 steps, seed 123) on PhysioNet 2016 only (no CirCor) in a separate copy of the repo (data/raw/repos/PCGKolect_pnonly); noise bank built with the repo's prep_noise.py from Bank-1 sources only. Reported as B6pn-a/B6pn-b, a sensitivity model outside the Holm family.
- Option A tie rule (user decision): wherever > 5 % of calibration normal beats tie at the matched (or pooled) threshold, all tied
  beats are rejected, so those cells reject 13.6-19.3 % of calibration beats instead of exactly 10 % (B6a/B6b on ZCHSound, B6pn on
  CirCor and ZCHSound). All other model x dataset cells (ties <= 2.6 %) keep exact-10 % randomised tie-breaking. Column
  `tie_rule` in results/c_thresholds.csv.

## Post-hoc analyses (2026-10-17): seed robustness and hybrid filters (see ANALYSIS_PLAN.md, "Post-hoc analyses")
- Base branch: the requested base `noise-fix` did not exist (local or origin); with the user's agreement `seeds-hybrid` was branched from master (after committing the B8 work), and the S1+S2-referenced E2 variant and `synthetic_regular` were implemented here.
- AGENT_PLAN's "one seed per deep model" rule is superseded for B2, B3, B4, B5, E6 (seeds 42, 43, 44) at the user's request; seed 42 = the original models, not retrained.
- B2/B3 seeds change only the random_state of the forests / gradient boosting / SVM Platt CV; B3's MI feature selection and SVM subsample stay at seed 42, so all three B3 seeds use the same 83 features.
- B1 (LR deterministic; SVM not reseeded), B6, B7 and the B8 variants are not reseeded; their per-seed rows are copies of seed 42, only their Holm-adjusted p-values change with the other models' seeds.
- E2s reference power = mean power of the clean window over its reference S1/S2 samples (segments fully inside the window, as used by B7; else the scored beat's own S1/S2), unfiltered like the original whole-window reference. At equal nominal SNR E2s noise is about 3 dB louder than E2 (S1+S2 power is ~3 dB above window power).
- synthetic_regular: same generator and source recordings as the two synthetic rhythm sets, appended last so their beats are bit-identical (checked); the synthetic set was re-scored by every model (scores of the old beats reproduce).
- Seed-robust verdict uses the covariate GEE (unadjusted / HR / HR + age) of seeds.py; pooled fit = same formula + C(seed) on the three seeds' stacked rejections, patient clusters (the three seeds' observations of a beat share the patient cluster). For deterministic models the pooled fit is their single fit. The pooled p-values are Holm-corrected across the same family. The independence-correlation sensitivity fit is kept for the canonical seed-42 files only.
- Hybrids are outside the main Holm family: each hybrid is added to the 11-model main family on its own (as B8v4/B8c), so the main models' verdicts do not depend on how many hybrids were built.
- H-AND/H-CASCADE default ("published") threshold = 0.9 on their percentile scale (= reject the top 10 % of clean normal calibration beats); used only in published-threshold rows.
- H-FUSE out-of-fold embeddings: fold-k deep models are trained on the other four folds with early stopping on fold k (the same protocol as the main model, whose early stopping used fold 0); the forest's nested validation uses out-of-fold features of other folds that came from deep models which saw the evaluated fold (standard stacking; second-order only).
- Hybrid Phase B metrics are 5-fold out-of-fold (B4/B5: fold 0 only), so they are not directly comparable to B4/B5's fold-0 numbers.
- E5 for hybrids: 3 filter seeds x 3 E5 seeds (9 runs per hybrid); existing filters keep their 3 E5 seeds at filter seed 42 (E5 was not part of the seed-robustness request).
- Training for the seed replicates ran with two jobs sharing the GPU for the first jobs, then one at a time; GPU non-determinism means a seed-42 retrain would not be bit-identical, which is why seed 42 reuses the original models.
- Pooled seed GEE uses independence working correlation instead of exchangeable (changed after seeing results): with three near-identical replicates per beat the exchangeable estimator weights patients and gave pooled ORs outside the per-seed range (B3 ZCHSound PFO 6.15 vs 3.44-3.78). Exchangeable pooled results kept as sensitivity columns. See ANALYSIS_PLAN.md.

## Post-hoc analysis (2026-10-17/18): debiased hybrids HD1-HD4 (branch hybrid-debias; see ANALYSIS_PLAN.md)
- Selection followed the pre-registered fallback: no candidate had both PhysioNet fold-0 gap CIs including 0 in >= 2 of 3 seeds; HD4 lambda 0.1 had the smallest summed |gap| among candidates within 0.01 AUROC of B4 (results/hd_selection.csv). Chosen before any test scoring.
- HD candidates are outside the main Holm family: each is added to the 11-model family on its own, as the earlier hybrids.
- HD1/HD2 use the percentile scale with default threshold 0.9 (as H-AND/H-CASCADE); used only in published-threshold rows.
- Phase B AUROC for HD candidates is on PhysioNet fold 0 (the selection set), as for B4/B5; the earlier hybrids' numbers are 5-fold out-of-fold.
- All HD candidates (not only the chosen one) were run through the full benchmark, for transparency; the read-out is for the chosen model only.
- E5 for HD candidates: 3 filter seeds x 3 E5 seeds.
- seeds-hybrid was merged into hybrid-debias before the analysis (ANALYSIS_PLAN.md conflict: both new sections kept).
